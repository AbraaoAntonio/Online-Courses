# Money-Making Ideas: 5 Proof-of-Concepts

Five product ideas, each with a **working, runnable proof-of-concept**,
built entirely from the skills already demonstrated in this repo:
scikit-learn (regression/classification), NLP (TF-IDF/Bag-of-Words),
pandas/data analysis, and Flask + Stripe deployment.

Every idea targets the same principle: **sell to someone who has money
and a problem right now**, not "build it and they will come." Read each
folder's README for the specific launch plan.

| # | Product | Folder | Who pays | Starting price |
|---|---------|--------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | ResumeFit — instant ATS resume/job match score | `1_resume_job_matcher/` | Job seekers | $9/report |
| 2 | InstantInsights — auto CSV/EDA report | `2_instant_csv_insights/` | Small biz, freelancers | $19/report |
| 3 | StayWise — churn prediction for subscription businesses | `3_churn_predictor/` | Gyms, SaaS, subscriptions | $99/mo |
| 4 | PricePilot — demand forecast & price recommendation | `4_dynamic_pricing/` | Etsy/Shopify sellers | $29/mo |
| 5 | CourseClip — auto show notes & quizzes from transcripts | `5_course_content_repurposer/` | Course creators, L&D teams | $15/video |

## Quick start — run any POC

```bash
cd money_making_ideas/<folder>
pip install -r requirements.txt
python <script>.py
```

Each folder is self-contained: the model/logic is in one script, sample
data is included, and a `README.md` explains the specific business plan.
Two of them (`1_resume_job_matcher`, `2_instant_csv_insights`) also
include a Flask `app.py` you can deploy directly as a live web tool.

## Since you're focused on paying down debt: which one first?

**Start with #1 or #2.** They are one-time-purchase, zero-recurring-cost
products you can sell to strangers on the internet within days — no
sales calls, no B2B trust-building required. Use the cash from the first
handful of sales to buy a little runway, then layer in #3 and #4, which
are recurring-revenue B2B products — slower to land the first customer,
but far more valuable once you have even 5-10 subscribers (that's
$500-$3,000/mo of recurring income from a tool that runs itself).

A realistic 90-day sequence:
1. **Week 1-2:** Ship #1 and #2 as live tools. Post proof (screenshots,
   before/after) where your target users already are — you don't need an
   audience, you need to go where the audience already exists (subreddits,
   Facebook groups, Fiverr/Upwork gigs).
2. **Week 2-6:** Use the income and validation from #1/#2 to fund your
   time. In parallel, run 10-20 free "audits" for #3 and #4 to land your
   first 3-5 recurring B2B customers.
3. **Week 6-12:** Reinvest into whichever product has the best
   payback — usually the recurring one (#3 or #4) — since predictable
   monthly revenue is what actually pays down debt reliably, while #1/#2/#5
   fund the runway and marketing to get there.

None of these alone is "the first million dollars" — that takes one of
them becoming a real, defensible product with hundreds of paying
customers. But all five are designed to get you real, external cash in
hand within days, which is the actual bottleneck when you're drowning in
debt: not a lack of ideas, but a lack of proof that strangers will pay.
Pick the one where you can name five real prospects today, and go sell to
them this week.
